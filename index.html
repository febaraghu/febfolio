About Me

I am a passionate Civil Engineering and BIM Software Trainer specializing in practical, industry-oriented training for aspiring engineers, graduates, and working professionals.

My expertise lies in delivering hands-on training in AutoCAD, Autodesk Revit, Civil 3D, Navisworks, Dynamo, COBie, Primavera P6, and BIM workflows.

I strongly believe that engineering education should go beyond theory. My training methodology focuses on live projects, real construction workflows, BIM standards, project planning, and practical implementation used in today's construction industry.

My goal is to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry expectations by helping learners build confidence through project-based learning and real-world applications.

Practical software expertise for real construction workflows. Every learning path is shaped around BIM standards, live project exercises, and career-ready confidence.
